A Utopian city insipired by Circuits

I was recently in New York. What an amazing city! I was walking on the streets of Manhattan, and I realized how efficient the city is. I was stunned by architecure, the busy vibe and ofcourse, all the beautiful women.

Note that I call the city efficeint. Its a strange way to describe a city. Infact, it almost felt like the city has life. One quick walk from the central park to the Macys store made me realize that cities do have life. It feels like as if the people walking on the streets are cells of one huge creature made of a concrete flesh connected through the very ground the people walk on. These tiny cells get absorbed and stored over night and they get circulated to a different organ when the sun is up. The system works as if the concrete organs are in agreement. The subways can be treated as veins, vessles or nerves. This is one way of looking at New York. A giant concrete creature that just uses human beings as cells who in turn add life to the city.

Another view point

Theres another way to look at the city. An ariel view. I meant it quite literally. Looking at the NY city from the airplane makes it look like a circuit. I am sure that people who have worked with circuits would have definitely thought of it. All the buildings resemble resistors and capacitors, and the flat laying buildings look like IC chips. It got me thinking, what if the city was built by a ciruit designer. Circuit deisgners alwasy think of the best way to arrange components with a basic principle that charge always takes the path with least resistance. Applying this analogy to a city, that would let them build.
